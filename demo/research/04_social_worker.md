# Sozialarbeiter:in (Social Worker)

**Sozialer Berater / Freiwilliger in einer Drittorganisation (Berlin)**
*Social Worker / Consultant / Volunteer in a Third Organization (Berlin)*

---

## Short Profile

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Social Worker / Counselor / Volunteer in a third-party organization (e.g., Caritas, Digital Zebra) |
| **Field of Work** | Advising clients who need/want to apply for basic security (and related benefits) |
| **Work Environment** | On-site counseling at a PC/terminal, often under time pressure, many parallel cases |
| **Work Principle** | "Help for self-help" (supported self-service) – collaboratively, structured, comprehensible (monitoring & feedback) |
| **Primary Channel** | PC/Web-first in consulting setting; mobile is supplementary (status/reminders) |

---

## Biography (Short)

The social worker (or counselor/volunteer) works in Berlin at a third-party organization and supports individuals like Sabine, Helmut, or Sandor through the basic security process. In the daily consulting routine at the PC/terminal, much is done under time pressure and with many parallel cases – therefore, a quick overview is crucial: What has been applied for, what is missing, what is time-critical, what status is currently applicable? Instead of "submitting a form," it's about helping people help themselves: structuring together, explaining, preparing evidence, conducting final checks, and – if necessary – submitting on time to avoid losing benefit months. A major challenge is unclear or missing documents (including foreign evidence) as well as housing costs/utility costs as a recurring stress point. The role requires a tool that supports co-use, status transparency, task lists, comments/notes, and a clear consent/role model, so that processes do not get stuck due to additional requests and media disruptions.

---

## Target Group Mix

Handles a variety of cases:

- Seniors with (low) or no digital skills
- Permanently disabled individuals with cognitive limitations (e.g., Sabine)
- People with refugee/migration experience, residence permits, complex documentation, and language barriers

---

## Context & Core Task

Not just "handing over a form," but:

- Guiding clients through the essential points in a structured manner
- Providing an overview: "What has been applied for?", "What is still missing?", "What is time-critical?"
- Step-by-step support: Follow-ups, deadlines, status changes, communication
- Utilizing expertise from many cases to avoid typical pitfalls (documents, KdU, EM, special cases)
- Systematically reducing delays: By preparing evidence early, conducting thorough final checks, and submitting on time, procedures should be processed faster.

**Additional Reality:** Many long-term recipients → renewals, change notifications (pension adjustments, housing cost changes) must be "easily repeatable."

---

## Wishes

- A tool that is not just a form, but a "Context & Deadline Coach."
- Targeted questions (context logic) instead of empty fields and generic full forms.
- Early evidence overview including "Why is it necessary?" + "How to obtain it?" for efficient preparation.
- Audit log/transparency of who did what (security regarding data & responsibilities).
- Portfolio overview for many parallel cases (filters, alerts, representation).
- Repeating/change processes finally fast and guided instead of "starting from scratch every time."
- PC/Web-first is perfect in the advisory setting; mobile is only supplementary (status/reminders).

## Fears & Insecurities

- Deadlines/monthly logic are missed → jeopardizing existence security.
- Missing evidence → requests for additional information → process stalls → risk of termination.
- Unclear status → distrust, double submissions, unnecessary inquiries.
- Lack of documentation → chaos during handovers/representation.
- Errors in foreign documents often lead to inquiries and delays.
- High time pressure + parallel cases → risk of "overlooking something".
- Concern that digital processes without clear roles/rights may lead to data protection or accountability issues.

## Pain Points and Barriers

- Heterogeneous target groups (languages, skills, special cases) → high individual effort.
- Paper files cause significant workload and media breaks.
- High complexity in income/rent costs/insurances/disability benefits/special cases.
- Document management is time-consuming (acquiring, checking, uploading, resubmitting).
- Co-usage on PCs is real, but many tools are not optimized for it.
- Transfers between teams/organizations/agencies are prone to errors without audits/notes.
- Repetitions/changes often start "from scratch" → constantly new sources of errors.
- Housing costs/rent generate the most inquiries (breakdowns, appropriateness).
- Inconsistent form versions & additional attachments by district.
- Lack of status transparency leads to distrust and unnecessary contacts.

---

## Central Hypotheses

1. When advisory consent-based access is available (Owner/Assist), media breaks and "uncontrolled sharing" of sensitive data decrease.
2. When a case cockpit clarifies status/next steps/deadlines, duplicate submissions and unnecessary inquiries decrease.
3. When contextual logic filters out irrelevant information and explains the "why," speed and understanding increase among diverse target groups.
4. When documents are uploaded with a quality check, follow-up requests decrease and processes speed up.
5. When timely submission is supported by the system (including to-dos), fewer performance months are lost.
6. When comments/notes are timestamped, handovers/representations work better and errors decrease.
7. When housing costs/KdU are implemented as a guided focus, follow-up questions and correction loops decrease significantly.
8. When portfolio management provides alerts, the risk of overlooking time-critical cases decreases.
9. When re-approval/change exists as its own flow (pre-filling), processing time per case decreases significantly.
10. When "questions first – form at the end" is implemented with review marking, completeness and data quality increase.

## Typical Starting Situation for Clients

- Uncertainty, fear of mistakes, overwhelm due to volume and bureaucratic language
- Low digital competence or limited cognitive capacity
- Documents are missing/unclear (old notices, abroad, translations, responsibilities)
- The process is multi-stage: follow-ups, appointments, deadlines, status often remains unclear
- Trust is fragile: need for legally secure confirmation and transparency

**Addition:** Many are long-term recipients: Reapplication/renewal requests, change notifications, pension adjustments, KdU changes are frequent and need to be "easily repeatable."

## Goals

### Primary Goals

- Submit applications on time and as complete as possible
- Avoid delays in procedures (prevent missing/unreadable/incorrect evidence)
- Offer targeted lists (audience-appropriate), provide early proactive hints, and show a progress display

### Secondary Goals

- Client understands: status, next steps, to-dos
- Utilize consultation time efficiently (less repetition, less media disruption)
- Clean documentation: What was explained? What is open? What is agreed?

### Long-term Goals

- Stabilize existence security (rent, needs)
- Clients become more self-effective over time
- Complete recurring cases faster (extensions/changes) to maintain capacity for new and complex cases
- Make handovers/representations functional (team/organization)

## Needs & Expectations for a Tool

### 1) Consent-based Access / Citizen Portal

- Login/view access to applications with client consent
- Roles: Client remains the owner; consulting has assist rights (read, comment, prepare)
- Dual usage case ("dual target group"): Citizens + professionals are central users who collaborate on the same case.

### 2) Status Transparency & Tracking

- Clear status: submitted / legally received / under review / follow-up / decision
- Next Steps: What is open? What is time-critical?
- System as a "context & deadline coach," not just a form: It explains why something is needed and by when.

### 3) Comments & Case Notes

- Comments in the application: "Consultation explained X", "Y is missing", "Z is being requested"
- Timestamped, transferable
- Notes must also work for recurring processes (e.g., "KDU change from date", "pension adjustment is available").

### 4) Proactive Assistant + Context Logic (Yes/No)

- Context-dependent logic: Depending on the target group, only relevant questions are displayed
- Irrelevant fields are hidden so that "empty fields" do not confuse (audience-appropriate)
- Show early: required evidence + why
- Actively support timely submission and tracking (clear flow + task list).

### 5) Documents / Error Hints

- "What else is missing?"-to-dos, upload check (readable/format)
- Hints on "how to obtain" + follow-up flow
- Error hints specifically for problematic evidence (e.g., documents from abroad: language, translation, incomplete information, incorrect formats).

### 6) Accessibility & Multilingualism

- Accessibility and multilingualism as significant leverage: simple language, read-aloud function, step-by-step mode, clear contrasts/readability.
- Target groups: older people, cognitive limitations, foreign individuals.

### 7) "Not App-First, but Consulting & PC-First"

- Main channel is PC/web in the consulting setting (offices, counseling centers). Mobile is supplementary (e.g., checking status, reminders), but not the primary way.

---

## Work Method / Journey of the Role

1. **Intake:** Categorize case, clarify urgency/deadlines
2. **Preparation:** Identify evidence, initiate procurement
3. **Joint Filling:** Wizard on PC with context logic
4. **Final Check:** Missing fields/documents, timely submission possible
5. **Follow-ups/Status:** Complete tasks, meet deadlines, check status
6. **Handover/Completion:** Documentation, next steps, possibly handover to another place

## User Stories

### Epic A – Access, Consent & Roles

#### User Story 1 – Consent-based Case Access (Citizen Portal)

**As a social worker/consultant,**
**I want** to access the client's application with their consent,
**so that** I can support them securely without uncontrolled data sharing.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Client can give consent (e.g., QR code/code/link) and revoke it at any time.
- I clearly see what rights I have (read/comment/uploads/prepare).
- There is an audit log showing who had access when and what was done.

#### User Story 2 – Assisted Mode (Co-usage)

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to work together with the client on the PC in "Assisted Mode,"
**so that** I can explain, structure, and avoid errors while keeping the client involved.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The application is usable PC/Web-first (consultation center/terminal).
- Progress is automatically saved; interruptions are possible.
- There is an audit log showing who had access when and what was done.

### Epic B – Case Cockpit: Status, Overview, Next Steps

#### User Story 3 – Case Overview (Status + Next Steps)

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to see the status and next steps at a glance,
**so that** I immediately know what to do and what is time-critical.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Status is clear: "submitted / legally received / under review / follow-up / decision".
- "Next Steps" show concrete tasks including deadlines and priorities.
- There is a list of "open / in progress / completed" per case.

#### User Story 4 – Overview of Ongoing Procedures (Portfolio)

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to manage multiple cases clearly in parallel,
**so that** I do not forget anything and handovers/representations work.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Filter/sorting by status, deadline, district, target group, urgency.
- Status is clear: "submitted / legally received / under review / follow-up / decision".
- "Next Steps" show concrete tasks including deadlines and priorities.

### Epic C – Context & Deadline Coach Instead of "Empty Form"

#### User Story 5 – Context Logic Hides Irrelevant Questions

**As a social worker,**
**I want** the system to display only relevant questions based on the target group,
**so that** clients are not confused by empty fields and we can proceed faster.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Yes/No logic hides irrelevant sections.
- The system briefly explains why a question is asked.

#### User Story 6 – Early Evidence Overview ("What Do You Need and Why?")

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to see early on which evidence is needed for this case (including justification),
**so that** I can prepare the consultation efficiently.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Automatic checklist based on target group/responses.
- Each piece of evidence includes "Why is it needed?" + "How to obtain it?".

### Epic D – Documents, Quality, Error Hints (Including Abroad)

#### User Story 7 – Document Upload with Quality Check

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to upload documents and receive immediate feedback on their quality,
**so that** unreadable or incorrect evidence does not lead to follow-ups.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The system recognizes/marks: unreadable, wrong format, incomplete.
- There are concrete suggestions for solutions ("re-photograph", "as PDF", "page 2 is missing").

#### User Story 8 – Error Hints for Evidence from Abroad

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to receive specific hints when evidence from abroad may be problematic,
**so that** inquiries and delays are reduced.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Hints for: language/translation, missing data, ambiguous amounts/time periods.
- The tool indicates what exactly is missing and what alternative is possible (e.g., "additional proof").

### Epic E – Timely Submission & Tracking

#### User Story 9 – Timely Submission Despite Missing Documents

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to enable timely submission even if documents are still missing,
**so that** clients do not lose benefit months and we can submit later.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Option: "timely submission & later supplement" is clearly available.
- The system briefly explains the monthly/deadline logic in simple language.
- After submission, I receive a to-do list for follow-ups including deadlines.

#### User Story 10 – Easily Manage Follow-ups

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to quickly process and submit follow-up requests,
**so that** the procedure does not stall.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Follow-up shows: "What is missing? By when? Where to upload?".
- Pre-filled from the last application (with clear change overview).

### Epic F – Renewal/Change Requests (Long-term Recipients)

#### User Story 11 – Simplifying Extensions & Change Notifications

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to quickly record renewals/changes (pension adjustments, KdU changes),
**so that** recurring processes do not start from scratch each time.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Pre-filled from the last application (with clear change overview).
- Guided change flow ("What has changed?") instead of a complete new application.

### Epic G – KdU/Wohnkosten & Health/Disability as Focus Areas

#### User Story 12 – Geführter KdU/Wohnkosten-Prozess (Guided Housing Costs Process)

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to record housing costs/KdU as a separate guided process (including moving/changes),
**so that** evidence is complete and KdU reviews generate fewer follow-up requests.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Structure: rent (cold/warm), utility costs, heating, hot water, changes/moving.
- Evidence per sub-area (rental agreement, current rent, heating costs etc.).
- Terms are explained ("What does that mean?") without blocking the flow.
- Hints "why relevant" + "typical errors" (e.g., missing breakdown).

#### User Story 13 – Strukturierte Erfassung bei EM/Behinderung (Structured Recording for Disability)

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to record expert opinions/disability certificates/rehab proofs in a structured manner,
**so that** cases with disability do not end up in unclear free-text fields.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Dedicated block "Health/Disability" with clear upload/proof fields.
- Option "not yet available – will be requested" + instructions.
- Review marks missing critical evidence.

### Epic H – Accessibility & Multilingualism (for Central Co-Use)

#### User Story 14 – Barrierefreier, mehrsprachiger Schritt-für-Schritt-Modus (Barrier-Free, Multilingual Step Mode)

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to be able to activate a barrier-free mode for clients,
**so that** they can participate better despite low competence/language barriers.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Simple language, read-aloud function, clear contrasts, short sections.
- Multilingual UI (at least core content/explanatory texts).

### Epic I – Documentation, Comments, Collaboration

#### User Story 15 – Case Notes & Comments Directly on the Application

**As a social worker,**
**I want** to be able to leave comments/notes on the application,
**so that** it is clear what has been discussed and what the next steps are.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Notes are timestamped and visible based on roles.
- Note types: "Explanation", "To-do", "Document requested", "Risk/Deadline".
- Notes are exportable or shareable for handovers (depending on data protection).

### Epic J – "Questions First, Official Form at the End" (Review & Conclusion)

#### User Story 16 – Review View: Form Only at the End, Colored Hints

**As a social worker,**
**I want** clients to see the "official" form only during a review phase,
**so that** we can work with understandable logic beforehand and make targeted corrections at the end.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The system first queries in simple logic and generates a form view at the end.
- Missing/unclear fields are color-coded and clickable ("jump to location").
- Attachments/documents are made visible as "provided" before submission.

---

## Central Product Logic

**"First answer questions – then official form":**

- All inquiries are initially answered in the tool (in simple logic).
- Only at the end is the "official" form shown as a filled view (review/check).
- Fields are color-coded that still need to be answered.
- Additionally, required attachments/forms/documents are displayed in time so they can be prepared.
- **Goal:** less delay, better completeness, faster processing by the administration.

- Diverse applicants require a high level of individual support
- High workload due to paper files
- High complexity (income, KdU, insurance, EM, special cases)
- Document management (acquiring, checking, uploading, following up)

- Repetitions and changes must function as guided, reusable processes

## Research Questions

- What minimal rights does "Assist" need (read/comment/upload/prepare) to ensure efficient consulting without data protection risks?
- How should consent (QR/code/link) be designed so that clients can understand and revoke it?
- What status levels and terminology are equally understandable for clients and consultants (including "legally received")?
- What next-step presentation (priority/deadline/to-do status) maximizes completion rates and minimizes consulting effort?
- Which documents most frequently cause quality issues (illegible/missing pages/incorrect time periods) and what feedback texts are effective?
- How should the foreign document notice be presented to be helpful (without legal advice, without overwhelming)?
- How should the KdU flow be structured (change/move/appropriateness/evidence) to ensure it runs smoothly in consulting situations?
- What data should be pre-filled for re-approval/change, and how is the change made transparent (change review)?
- What portfolio filters/warning logic align with the actual workload (72h/7 days, urgency, district, target group)?
- How should notes/audit logs be designed to ensure handovers between organization/team/authority truly work?

## Product Requirements

- Consent & Role Model: Client remains the Owner; consultation receives Assist rights (Read/Comment/Uploads/Preparation) + Revocation + Audit Log.
- Assisted Mode (Co-usage): PC/Web-first, autosave, interruptions, collaborative work without loss of control.
- Case Cockpit: clear status + Next Steps with deadlines/priorities + task lists per case.
- Portfolio View: manage parallel cases (filter by status/deadline/district/target group/urgency + alerts).
- Comments & Case Notes: timestamped, transferable, also for recurring processes (change of KdU, pension adjustment).
- Context Logic (Yes/No): only relevant questions; explanation "why"; evidence directly in the section.
- Document Management: checklists/to-dos, upload quality check, follow-up flow, assignment + comment in one flow.
- Special error notes for abroad: language/translation/time periods/ambiguous amounts, including proposed solutions (note, no legal advice).
- Deadline-compliant submission: clear mode + explanation of the monthly logic + automatic follow-up to-dos.
- Repetition/Change flows: pre-filled from the last application, guided change flow, automatic update checklist.
- KdU as a guided process: rent → utilities → heating → hot water; proofs per step; icons/examples.
- "Questions first, official form at the end": review with highlighted open fields.
- Multilingualism & simple language: switchable at any time; difficult terms explained; optional read-aloud/step mode.
- Supported self-service in a consulting setting: PC/web-first stable; save/continue; follow-up without restart; optional access to consulting centers with consent.

## Success Criteria

- Fewer delays due to missing/incorrect evidence
- More timely submissions + clear follow-up to-dos
- Faster repeat/change processes (renewal, pension adjustment, KdU changes)
- Clients + consultants have an overview at all times: status, next steps, open points
- Barrier-free use in the consulting setting (PC-first, multilingual)

- Assisted Mode + Consent/Role Model + Audit Log
- PC/Web-first, Mobile supplementary (status/reminder)
- Context & deadline coach (Yes/No, why/when)
- Housing costs/KdU as a separate guided focus (relocation/change/evidence)
- EM/disability: structured recording (expert opinions, rehab, EM notice)
- Document management with quality/error hints (including abroad)
- "Questions first, form at the end" + colored review marking
